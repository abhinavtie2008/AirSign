Rules of the air

AirSign reads hand landmarks, not screen coordinates. These three ideas keep it predictable.

01 Different shapes, different jobs

Pointing and pinching share an index finger, but a fist is unmistakable. That's why drag never fires a stray click on the way in.

02 Your hand sets the pace

Cursor smoothing slows down when you hold still and opens up when you move fast, so pointing feels steady without feeling laggy.

03 Everything stays here

The camera feed, the model and the 21 landmarks are processed in your browser on this device. No network request carries video.
