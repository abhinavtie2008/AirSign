How it works

Scroll with two fingers to read on.

01

Hand landmarks

MediaPipe Hands finds 21 points on your hand in every webcam frame, entirely in the browser.

02

Smooth cursor

The index fingertip is mapped to the screen through a comfortable active zone, then smoothed with a One Euro filter so it stays steady when you pause and responsive when you move fast.

03

Pinch to click

Thumb and index tip are compared against your hand size, so it works at any distance. The cursor locks in place the instant you pinch and fires one real click right away — nothing to hold, nothing that can be mistaken for a drag.

04

Fist to drag

Click and drag are different hand shapes on purpose, so AirSign never has to guess which one you meant. Close your whole hand into a fist over something and it's grabbed; move your hand and it follows, tracked by the palm's own movement since a fist has no extended fingertip to point with. Open your hand again to let go — useful for the slider below, draggable cards, anything a mouse could drag.

05

Two-finger scroll

Raise index and middle fingers. Move your hand above the starting point to scroll up, below it to scroll down. The further you go, the faster it moves.

06

Real clicks, real pages

AirSign finds the element under the cursor and dispatches genuine mouse events, so it works on any page you embed it in.

07

Private by design

No frames leave your device. Tracking runs locally on CPU or GPU.
